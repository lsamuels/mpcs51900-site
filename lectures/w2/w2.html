    Week 2: Strings, Pointers, and Memory¶

    We finish strings from last week, then move on to pointers and managing memory yourself.

    • Strings: char arrays ending in '\0', passing strings to functions, and <string.h>

    • Pointers: addresses, & and *, NULL, and common pointer pitfalls

    • Pointers and functions: call by value, passing pointers to change a caller’s variables, and returning multiple values

    • Pointers and arrays: array-to-pointer conversion, pointer arithmetic, and comparing strings

    • Dynamic memory: the stack vs. the heap, malloc and free, dangling pointers, memory leaks, and pointers to structs

    • Command-line arguments: argc, argv, and the environment

    Reading¶

    K.N. King, C Programming: A Modern Approach, chapters 11, 12, 13, and 17.
