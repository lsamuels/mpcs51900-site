    Week 3: Pointers to Pointers and Building Programs¶

    Overview¶

    This week finishes our tour of pointers and dynamic memory, then looks at how real C programs are split across files and built with make.

    Topics¶

    • Enumerations, and catching non-standard C with -pedantic

    • Pointers to pointers and changing a caller’s pointer

    • Arrays of strings, argv, and 2D arrays (automatic and dynamic)

    • realloc, calloc, memcpy, memmove, and memset

    • Header files, include guards, and separate compilation

    • Reading link errors, and linking the math library with -lm

    • Linkage with extern and static

    • Forward-declared types and encapsulation with opaque structs

    • Makefiles: rules, variables, pattern rules, .PHONY, and all

    • A typical C project layout
