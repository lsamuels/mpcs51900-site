Week 8: Signals
===============

This week covers signals, the simplest form of interprocess communication (IPC).

* What IPC is, and where signals fit among pipes, files, shared memory and sockets
* What a signal is: its cause, its default action, and the common signals
* Pending and blocked signals, and why signals are not queued
* Process groups, foreground and background jobs, and job control (``fg``, ``bg``, ``jobs``)
* Sending signals with ``kill``, ``raise`` and ``alarm``, and from the keyboard (ctrl-c, ctrl-z)
* Installing handlers with ``signal`` and ``sigaction``, and how handlers behave across ``fork`` and ``exec``
* Blocking signals with ``sigprocmask``
* Writing safe handlers: async-signal-safe functions, ``volatile sig_atomic_t`` flags, and ``EINTR``
